How it works

Three steps. Zero spreadsheets.

CoinSafe does the math, so you don't have to.

  1. Step 1

    Set your monthly budget

    Tell CoinSafe how much you want to spend this month — for example, “my budget is $2,000.”

  2. Step 2

    Text your expenses

    Type it the way you'd tell a friend: “spent 35 on uber” or “coffee 4.50.” The AI picks up the amount and category.

  3. Step 3

    Get your daily summary

    Every evening, CoinSafe sends a quick recap of what you spent and the % of your monthly budget already used.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is CoinSafe free?

CoinSafe is in early access and free to try. If we ever introduce paid plans, we'll tell you clearly before anything changes.

Which messaging apps does it work with?

Right now, CoinSafe works on Telegram only. WhatsApp, iMessage and Messenger are coming soon — join the waitlist to hear when your app is ready.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. You simply tell CoinSafe what you spent by sending a message. It doesn't ask for your bank login or card numbers.

Is my data safe?

We aim to collect only what's needed to track your spending and send your summaries, and we're continuing to improve how we protect it. Please avoid sharing sensitive information like passwords or full card numbers in the chat. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

Is CoinSafe financial advice?

No. CoinSafe's tips are general, educational information to help you build better money habits. For decisions about investing, taxes or debt, talk to a qualified professional.
